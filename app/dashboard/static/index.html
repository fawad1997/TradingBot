News trading — orders placed either side of big economic releases
Before a high-impact release the bot places one buy order above the price and one sell order below it. Whichever the news triggers becomes the trade; the other is cancelled automatically. It stands down if the buy/sell gap blows out beforehand.
Self-adjusting settings — how the bot retunes itself each hour
Each hour the bot re-measures how much each symbol is moving and how wide its buy/sell gap usually is, then adjusts the news-order distances and the gap ceiling to match. Position sizes and risk limits are never adjusted this way — those stay exactly where you set them.
Market hours — when each symbol opens and closes
Weekend reopen times are estimates from the configured schedule. Your broker's actual hours, holidays and individual contract sessions may differ.
